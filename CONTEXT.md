# Campus Vault

Campus Vault is USSTM's inventory access context: members discover items, request or borrow them, and coordinate pickup or return with responsible organizers.

## Language

**Campus Vault**:
The USSTM inventory access service for borrowing, taking, and managing shared items.
_Avoid_: CV, inventory app, vault app

**Member**:
A person who belongs to a USSTM group and can use that group's shared items.
_Avoid_: User, customer, borrower

**Group Admin**:
A member who manages people and operational data for one USSTM group.
_Avoid_: Club admin, group manager

**Approver**:
A person responsible for reviewing high-value item requests and coordinating approval-sensitive operations across groups.
_Avoid_: Reviewer, VP Operations

**Global Admin**:
A person with system-wide responsibility for Campus Vault configuration and administration.
_Avoid_: Super admin, system owner

**Catalog**:
The browsable set of shared items that members can take, borrow, or request through Campus Vault.
_Avoid_: Inventory list, store, marketplace

**Cart**:
A member's temporary selection of items for a specific group before checkout.
_Avoid_: Basket, order, request cart

**Active Group**:
The group a member is currently acting within for catalog, cart, checkout, and group-scoped workflows.
_Avoid_: Selected organization, tenant, club context

**Take Item**:
An item a member can take without return tracking.
_Avoid_: Low item, consumable

**Borrow Item**:
An item a member borrows and is expected to return.
_Avoid_: Medium item, loan item

**Request Item**:
An item a member must request and receive approval for before access.
_Avoid_: High item, restricted item

**Checkout Review**:
The step where a member confirms cart contents grouped by whether items will be taken, borrowed, or requested.
_Avoid_: Order review, purchase review

**My Activity**:
The member-facing area for tracking bookings, borrowings, and requests.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, history, account activity

**Booking**:
A scheduled pickup or return coordination between a member and a responsible organizer.
_Avoid_: Appointment, reservation

**Borrowing**:
A record that a member has an item out or has returned it.
_Avoid_: Loan, rental

**Request**:
A member's request for approval to access a Request Item.
_Avoid_: Application, approval ticket

**Approval**:
An Approver's decision to approve or deny a Request.
_Avoid_: Review, authorization
